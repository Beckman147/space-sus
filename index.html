SPACE SUS

A fan-made social deduction game. Play online with friends — no accounts, no installs.

How to play: Crewmates finish tasks (walk to a glowing console, press E). One player is secretly the Impostor and must eliminate the crew without getting caught. Report bodies or hit the emergency button to call a meeting and vote someone out. Move with WASD / arrows — or on phones and tablets, touch and drag anywhere on the map to move. Best with 4–10 players.
